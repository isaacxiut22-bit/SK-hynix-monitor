Scope, sources & coverage limits

Federal docket search

CourtListener/RECAP party-name search for SK hynix, Hynix Semiconductor and Solidigm; requires a private CourtListener API token. Search matches are flagged for review of entity and defendant role. RECAP coverage and indexing can be incomplete and delayed. No paid PACER connection.

State and federal filing reports

Google News RSS searches for public reports of lawsuits and complaints involving SK hynix. These are not court-filing records and may describe previously existing matters, other defendants, or non-U.S. proceedings. Free state court portals are linked above for manual searches; no automated nationwide state court coverage is claimed.

Plaintiff-firm monitoring

Google News queries for plaintiff announcements and direct checks of public listing pages at Kessler Topaz, Rosen Law, and Hagens Berman when permitted by robots.txt. Direct pages may use JavaScript or omit private solicitations. Each lead is labeled unverified and must be checked at the source.

Reading the record

Historical baseline entries were gathered November 19, 2026. Media reports, direct firm listings and CourtListener docket hits remain distinct; none proves the company is a defendant or a new action was filed. Related law-firm notices are grouped conservatively by identified firm, investigation subject and publication period, with original source links retained. Ambiguous or distinct matters remain separate for manual review. Scheduled checks are best-effort, and failures are shown by source. A successful scan with no findings does not imply no filings exist.
